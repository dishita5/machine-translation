# Qualitative Translation Examples

## Overview

This document presents representative English-to-Spanish translations generated by the Basic Transformer and TinyLlama models. The examples were selected from their recorded 1,000-example test prediction files.

Individual prediction files for mT5, OPUS-MT, and Gemma were not available for this qualitative comparison. Their quantitative results are reported separately in `final_model_comparison_detailed.csv`.

## Translation Examples

### Example 1

**English input:** I know what the lies were.

**Reference:** Sé cuáles eran las mentiras.

- **Basic Transformer:** `lo que lo que lo que lo que lo que lo que...`
- **TinyLlama:** Siento lo que fueron las mentiras.

**Observation:** The Basic Transformer generated a repetitive sequence. TinyLlama produced a grammatical-looking sentence but did not preserve the intended meaning accurately.

### Example 2

**English input:** This is not possible for a man of flesh and blood.

**Reference:** Esto no es posible para un hombre de carne y hueso.

- **Basic Transformer:** `esto no es una <unk> y no es una <unk>`
- **TinyLlama:** No es posible para un hombre de sangre y carne.

**Observation:** The Basic Transformer produced unknown tokens and an incomplete translation. TinyLlama preserved the general meaning, although its rendering of the idiomatic expression differed from the reference.

### Example 3

**English input:** - Want help?

**Reference:** - ¿Quieres ayuda?

- **Basic Transformer:** `- <unk>`
- **TinyLlama:** - ¿Quieres ayuda?

**Observation:** TinyLlama reproduced the reference accurately, while the Basic Transformer failed to generate a meaningful translation.

## Discussion

The examples illustrate differences in output quality between the two available prediction sets. The custom Transformer frequently generated `<unk>` tokens and repetitive text, suggesting limitations in its learned vocabulary representations and sequence generation.

TinyLlama generated more readable outputs and correctly translated some short sentences. However, readable output did not always guarantee semantic accuracy.

These examples are qualitative illustrations, not a substitute for the quantitative evaluation across the full test set. BLEU and chrF results for all five systems are provided separately in the main comparison results.
